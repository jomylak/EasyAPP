- DATE FIELDS: all three patterns from the core prompt's DATE FIELD PROTOCOL have been confirmed on Workday, sometimes for different fields on the SAME application -- always classify each date field individually, never assume one fix carries over to the next date field on the same form.
  * Pattern 1 (typeable field, browser_type slowly commits): confirmed on some tenants' availability/start-date field (e.g. a plain "9/16/2026" field accepted a normal type with no fight at all).
  * Pattern 2 (spinbutton triplet): confirmed on the Self-Identify/disability-signature date field and on the Education "first year attended" field. Selector patterns seen: `*-dateSectionMonth-input`, `*-dateSectionYear-input`, `role="spinbutton"`. Typing the digits alone (no explicit Tab needed) reliably auto-advances across month/day/year segments. Confirmed independently by two different models on two different runs.
  * Pattern 3 (calendar-popup, no typeable input): confirmed on a start-date field with nothing to type into at all -- required clicking a calendar icon, then "Next month" repeatedly to reach the target month, then clicking the target day number. A model that doesn't recognize "calendar popup, no adjacent text input" as the trigger for this pattern will keep retrying text entry and burn many turns before finding the fix by accident -- recognize it immediately instead.
- Field of Study (multi-select combobox on Education section): filtered results can render in the page's actual DOM but not show up correctly in the accessibility-tree snapshot, and pressing Enter can commit the wrong top-of-list option instead of your intended match (e.g. selecting "Accountancy" when you typed a computer science query). Verified fix, tested by hand: click into the field, type a short distinctive query like "computer sci" (not the full exact major name), then press Enter -- it correctly autofilled "Computer and Information Sciences". Manually confirmed correct by the user. Do NOT use Delete-to-clear-then-click-a-text-selector as a fix -- that was a model's own workaround for the same symptom and is unnecessarily convoluted next to just typing a shorter/looser query and hitting Enter.
